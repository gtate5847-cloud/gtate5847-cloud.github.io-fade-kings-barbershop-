Who We Are

More Than a Haircut

Fade Kings was built on precision, not shortcuts — every cut is done by a barber who treats your fade like it's their own reputation on the line. No corporate scripts, no rushed appointments, just skilled hands, straight razors, and a shop that feels like home. We've built our name on consistency, so you leave looking sharp every single time.
